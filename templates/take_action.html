Take action

Beautiful ocean needs mindful people.

Change is possible when science, policy, and daily choices move in the same direction.

One decade, one ocean

Protect 30% of the ocean by 2030.

The Ocean Decade gives every local action a global frame. Start with practical steps that lower pressure on marine habitats and help people understand what is at stake.

Protect habitats Choose lower-impact seafood Share ocean knowledge Join local events

Problem 1: Overfishing

Ask where your seafood comes from.

Choose verified sustainable options, reduce waste, and support rules that keep fish populations above recovery thresholds.

Problem 2: Deep sea pressure

Protect places before extracting from them.

Deep ocean ecosystems recover slowly. Follow proposed mining permits, share credible research, and back a precautionary approach.

Problem 3: Pollution

Stop waste before it reaches the water.

Support deposit systems, refill habits, storm-drain cleanups, and stronger limits on chemical and plastic discharge.

Problem 4: Restoration

Make room for habitats to return.

Help restore dunes, shellfish reefs, kelp forests, and salt marshes that protect coastlines and store carbon.
