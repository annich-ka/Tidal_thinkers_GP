Plastic Pollution

Every year 19–23 million tonnes of plastic waste leaks into aquatic ecosystems. Plastic pollution can alter natural processes, reducing ecosystems' ability to adapt to climate change, directly affecting millions of people's livelihoods.

Deep Sea Mining

Scientists warn that deep sea mining can cause irreversible biodiversity loss, disrupt fragile marine ecosystems, and release toxins into the water.

Bottom Trawling

What is it?

Bottom trawling is the act of dragging heavy nets or metal frames along the seabed to catch fish and shellfish.

It destroys seabed habitats, reduces biodiversity, and causes high rates of bycatch, meaning catching unintended species like crustaceans, sea turtles, and fish.

What can you do?

Buy second-hand electronics

Refurbished phones and laptops reduce the need for newly mined materials.

Extend the life of your current electronics

The longer you keep your devices, the less electronic waste ends up polluting our oceans.
