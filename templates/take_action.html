Plastic Pollution

What can you do?

Join or Create a Clean Up

Join municipal clean-up events, or take the initiative and organize your own campaign.

Adopt a circular lifestyle

Choose reusable over disposable and reduce both plastic waste and CO₂ emissions.

Make sure your toiletries are plastic-free

Toiletries are a major source of microplastics, which get washed into the oceans straight from our bathrooms.

Spot the logo and select alternatives

It's a reminder that this product contains plastic that could end up in the ocean.

Deep Sea Mining

What can you do?

Buy second-hand electronics

Refurbished phones and laptops reduce the need for newly mined materials.

Extend the life of your current electronics

The longer you keep your devices, the less electronic waste ends up polluting our oceans.

Bottom Trawling

What is it?

Bottom trawling is the act of dragging heavy nets or metal frames along the seabed to catch fish and shellfish.

It destroys seabed habitats, reduces biodiversity, and causes high rates of bycatch, meaning catching unintended species.

What can you do?

Did you know?

All unprocessed fish sold in the EU have to display the category of fishing gear used to capture them. This is not compulsory for processed seafood, such as canned, cooked or smoked products.

What can you do?

When shopping, check for these logos. These certifications mean the fishery or farm has met certain environmental standards.

Certified doesn't mean perfect.

MSC fish isn't always low-bycatch, and the label doesn't guarantee full sustainability. It means the fishery is better managed than most. Use it as a starting point.
