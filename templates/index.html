The state of the ocean

The oceans were at the origin of life.

They still provide the basic conditions of our life on earth. The UN Ocean Decade turns that knowledge into a shared deadline for protection, restoration, and better choices.

The UN sees this decade as an opportunity.

By 2030, at least 30% of our oceans must be protected.

Curious to know more?

Watch the documentary Our Oceans for the beauty and urgency of the sea.

Four pressure points

Beautiful oceans have these four ugly problems

Mineral extraction can disturb habitats that formed over thousands of years and are still barely understood.

Taking more than ecosystems can replace weakens food webs and leaves coastal communities more exposed.

Plastic, chemical runoff, and noise move through the water long after the original source is gone.

Heavy gear can scrape the seabed, damage carbon-rich habitats, and reduce recovery for marine life.
